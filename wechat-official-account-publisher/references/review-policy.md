# Editorial and compliance review

## Evidence

- Trace factual claims, numbers, quotations, and attributed opinions to source IDs.
- Prefer official documents, original research, company filings, and direct statements.
- Mark estimates and model-generated interpretations as such.
- Remove unresolved claims or rewrite them as explicit uncertainty.

## Copyright and attribution

- Synthesize sources; do not reproduce substantial passages or imitate a living author's distinctive style.
- Quote only what is necessary and attribute it.
- Confirm image licenses or use user-owned/generated assets.

## Risk review

Flag personal data, defamation risk, confidential material, guarantees, regulated advice, misleading promotions, and claims involving minors. High-severity issues block submission.

## AI disclosure

Determine the applicable disclosure based on the content, account, audience, and current platform/legal requirements. Do not remove or conceal provenance metadata. The default bundle records `ai_generated: true` and includes a visible disclosure for human review. If current rules are unclear or the subject is consequential, require a human compliance decision before submission.

## Approval boundary

The reviewer may approve the content package, but only the user can authorize an external draft-box write. Public publishing is never authorized by this skill.
